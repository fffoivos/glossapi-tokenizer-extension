# 07 · scripts — the fail-closed control plane

> **In one line:** 32 Python entry points that validate the recipe, build the single authoritative launch gate, choose the execution profile, supervise each segment, and report exactly how far the campaign has got.
> **Period:** 2026-08-05 → 2026-08-09. **Status:** completed; the campaign they drove finished on 2026-08-11.
> **Came from / led to:** [`../configs/`](../configs/) → these validators and gates → [`../clariden/`](../clariden/) job submissions → the campaign completion receipt.

## Why this existed

Nothing about this run was allowed to be authorized by a human assertion. A production launch had to be a *derived* artifact: one launch gate built from a complete set of receipts, each receipt produced by a job that itself revalidated its inputs. The same principle applies mid-run — a segment advances only from a signed permit over a verified checkpoint.

## Groups

**Validators (run before anything).** [`validate_recipe.py`](validate_recipe.py) checks the scientific geometry and the optional frozen data receipts; [`validate_execution_profile.py`](validate_execution_profile.py) validates a 16- or 32-node profile *without changing science*; [`validate_megatron_runtime.py`](validate_megatron_runtime.py) revalidates the patched Megatron tree against its frozen receipt before every job; [`validate_owner_decisions.py`](validate_owner_decisions.py) checks the D0/libduth/launch decisions; [`verify_hf_visibility.py`](verify_hf_visibility.py) receipts the public visibility and exact revision of the training dataset. [`contract.py`](contract.py) holds the shared receipt helpers.

**Profile selection.** [`freeze_benchmark_contract.py`](freeze_benchmark_contract.py) freezes the exact D0 prefix shared by the 16- and 32-node benchmarks. [`finalize_parallelism_benchmark.py`](finalize_parallelism_benchmark.py) promotes DP64 only after numerical, restart, cursor **and** speed gates pass — it did not: DP64 was rejected on trajectory drift despite being faster. [`finalize_checkpoint_parity_smoke.py`](finalize_checkpoint_parity_smoke.py) freezes a two-allocation DP32 *synchronous*-checkpoint restart proof, added after async-save evidence was invalidated. [`finalize_dp32_fallback_selection.py`](finalize_dp32_fallback_selection.py) is the independently fail-closed no-rebenchmark path: it accepts only the declared `dp32_16node` fallback after a single-leaf 162-update control plus two synchronous iteration-160 restarts, and its receipt records `candidate_evaluated: false` / `candidate_promoted: false` so the gate validates semantics rather than a file name.

**Smokes and gates.** [`finalize_graceful_stop_smoke.py`](finalize_graceful_stop_smoke.py) proves `B:USR1` saved a checkpoint and that resume advanced (stop at update 11, resume to 12); [`finalize_conversion_smoke.py`](finalize_conversion_smoke.py) freezes the checkpoint→HF→GreekMMLU smoke; [`freeze_initial_checkpoint.py`](freeze_initial_checkpoint.py) hashes the exact legacy TP2 initialization tree; [`submit_nested_sbatch_probe.py`](submit_nested_sbatch_probe.py) submits a real child job from the supervisor's exact uenv runtime, the proof that nested submission works under the pinned image. [`capture_launch_environment.py`](capture_launch_environment.py) captures storage and scheduler evidence immediately before launch, and [`build_launch_gate.py`](build_launch_gate.py) builds the sole authoritative gate. [`audit_submitted_job_resources.py`](audit_submitted_job_resources.py) fails closed when a submitted job uses the wrong Clariden resources.

**Sanitized rebind (08-07/08).** [`derive_sanitized_contracts.py`](derive_sanitized_contracts.py) derives the exact full-run recipe and execution profiles from the sanitized data — the source of the 76,685,490,476-token / 18,284-update horizon. [`successor_semantic_identity.py`](successor_semantic_identity.py), [`compare_successor_stage.py`](compare_successor_stage.py), [`rebind_selected_execution_profile.py`](rebind_selected_execution_profile.py) and [`build_successor_launch_gate.py`](build_successor_launch_gate.py) together prove that a successor stage changes only receipt/path bindings, which is what allowed the 330 GB packed payload to be reused rather than rebuilt.

**Running the campaign.** [`supervise_campaign.py`](supervise_campaign.py) and its resource-aware successor [`supervise_campaign_resource_aware.py`](supervise_campaign_resource_aware.py) gate one segment, recover infrastructure failures and continue. [`prequeue_next_segment.py`](prequeue_next_segment.py) implements the one-audited-delayed-holder policy from [`../configs/prequeue_schedule_8b.json`](../configs/prequeue_schedule_8b.json). [`transition_pending_supervisor.py`](transition_pending_supervisor.py) atomically replaces a *pending* supervisor with a newer bundle — audit first, cancel second — and [`reconstruct_legacy_supervisor_receipt.py`](reconstruct_legacy_supervisor_receipt.py) rebuilds the one v19-era receipt that was missing, with no `sbatch`, `scancel` or training action of its own. [`campaign_status.py`](campaign_status.py) reports the exact completed update/token boundary, Slurm states and required-versus-completed receipts; [`finalize_training.py`](finalize_training.py) and [`finalize_campaign.py`](finalize_campaign.py) close the evidence set.

**Retention watch.** [`analyze_retention_snapshot.py`](analyze_retention_snapshot.py) freezes learning and retention alerts from one training log against the pre-registered thresholds (+0.05 nats twice = warning, +0.08 or +0.05 macro = critical), and [`forecast_retention_slope.py`](forecast_retention_slope.py) fits a read-only drift forecast. Both are reporting instruments — neither can stop training.

## Outcome

- One launch gate, one selected profile (`dp32_16node`), five audited segments, and a completion receipt binding 19 GreekMMLU results, 39 per-document receipts, 5 training-attempt audits, the launch gate, the profile and the terminal export.
- The most-revised idea here is *how to hold a scarce allocation without wasting it*: from "no happy-path successor may be prequeued" to "at most one audited, delayed, permit-gated successor", with the hold derived from the target segment's runtime and the trigger from the source segment's conservative budget.
