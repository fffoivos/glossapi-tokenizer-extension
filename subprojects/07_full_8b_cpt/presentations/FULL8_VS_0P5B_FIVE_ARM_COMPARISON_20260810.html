03 · Source-conditioned learning

Greek loss falls at both scales

Each panel plots BPB change from that run’s first available source-loss measurement. This normalization compares trajectory shape while avoiding a false claim that the two packed heldout geometries are identical. Negative values indicate learning.

What transfers: D0 produces broad reductions on HPLT, GlossAPI, specialist Greek, polytonic material, and neutral external Greek at both scales. The 8B curves generally descend faster in active-token space, consistent with greater capacity—not proof that the sanitized corpus is intrinsically better.

04 · Retention and forgetting

Replay loss rises while Old Greek continues learning

Forgetting is plotted as current BPB minus the best BPB observed earlier in the same trajectory. Zero means the panel is at its best-so-far value. The complete trajectories are shown; no tail crop or smoothing is used.

Shared pattern: English, code, mathematics, German, Russian, and Chinese all rise from their best values under D0 at both scales, while Old Greek keeps improving because it receives the fixed 1% replay stream. At the current 8B snapshot, Chinese has the largest rise-from-best (+0.0724 BPB), followed by German (+0.0498) and English (+0.0344).

05 · Interpretation

The 0.5B screen supports D0; it does not guarantee monotonic 8B benchmarks

Convergent evidence

What replicated conceptually

  • D0 remains the least assumption-heavy schedule and was the 0.5B observed all-round leader after retention screening.
  • Both scales learn every Greek source family under stationary mixing.
  • Both scales show non-monotonic GreekMMLU NLL: all five 0.5B endpoints are worse than their own best-NLL checkpoint, as is the current 8B point.
  • The 8B run’s benchmark separation is mostly model capacity: at matched ~60B tokens it is +12.42 accuracy points and −0.1755 NLL versus 0.5B D0.
Non-identification

What cannot be attributed

  • 8B versus 0.5B is not a controlled scale-only comparison: architecture, tied/untied TD, absolute LR, global batch, and sanitized corpus differ.
  • None of the 13 fast source panels has an exact target-count/byte geometry match across scales; absolute cross-scale BPB ranks are descriptive.
  • The current 8B benchmark endpoint is not the run endpoint: it is update 14,304 / 59.995B evaluated tokens, immediately before cooldown.
  • No claim of statistical superiority across model scales is needed; the observed margins are point estimates from distinct training contracts.

Best current reading: the 8B run has learned materially more Greek multiple-choice discrimination than any 0.5B arm, but its best observed GreekMMLU checkpoint remains around 40B tokens. The subsequent NLL rise mirrors the late rebound already visible in every 0.5B schedule, so the next cooldown checkpoints are diagnostic rather than merely ceremonial.
