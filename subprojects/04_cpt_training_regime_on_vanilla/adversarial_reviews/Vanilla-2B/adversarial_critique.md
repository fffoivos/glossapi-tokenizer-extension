**C2. The matched-config Apertus-Base eval is the right answer to the geometry confound *in principle*, but the BPB output suggests the implementation perturbs the model in practice.** Matched-config Greek BPB (`/capstor/scratch/cscs/fffoivos/runs/04_vanilla_cpt/eval_apertus_base_matched_rope500k_seq4096/heldout_greek_bpb.json`) reports `global bpb=1.2216` with per-source `greek_academic=1.6774`, `greek_dialogue_textbooks=1.5717`, `greek_legal_civic=1.3137`, `greek_hplt_clean60=0.7215`. The likely explanation: the matched-config staging dir (`/iopsstor/.../apertus-8b-2509-matched-rope500k-seq4096`, manifest at `MANIFEST.json`) overrides only `rope_theta=500000`, `max_position_embeddings=4096`, `rope_scaling=null` while symlinking the original `model-*.safetensors`. Apertus-Base was pretrained at `rope_theta=12000000` per the §2.3/§2.5 long-context recipe — the trained KV/query interactions assume that wavelength. Forcing inference-time `rope_theta=500K` rotates K/Q by a different phase per token position, so the model is evaluated under positional embeddings it never saw. This is consistent with Greek BPB blowing up while leaving English ARC/HellaSwag (token positions short, position confusion local) less affected. **Implication:** the matched-config eval is **not** a free re-anchor of Apertus-Base; it is itself a perturbation. Before treating it as the gold baseline, sanity-check by also evaluating matched-config Apertus-Base on a positional-insensitive English task (e.g., XNLI_en) and comparing against the published Apertus-Base XNLI_en number — if the matched-config eval significantly underperforms there too, the geometry override has globally degraded the model and is not the apples-to-apples baseline the design claims. The 5B report must either (a) document this caveat explicitly, (b) re-run Apertus-Base at its native Path-A geometry and compare iter 477 *at Path B vs. Apertus-Base at Path A* as the headline (acknowledging the confound), or (c) re-train iter-477 worth of Vanilla CPT at Path A — which the plan explicitly defers to Task 2 production.

**C3. No decontamination evidence for any public Greek MCQ benchmark vs `hplt_b1_5b.jsonl`.** This is unchanged from Vanilla-0.5B Critical-3 and Vanilla-1B Critical-3. **8. Regime Hypothesis Verdict**

**Read at iter 477 (post-warmup, first stable-LR snapshot, 2.001B tokens):**

The evidence is **directionally consistent with "the corrected regime helps materially over the bakeoff regime"** but **not yet sufficient to conclude "the corrected regime recovers to Apertus-Base"** for these reasons:

**Pro-regime-fix evidence (strong, conditional):**

1. **Native MCQ headline (3-task, V4 bootstrap CIs, 95 % percentile, 1000 resamples):**
   - bakeoff-Vanilla-2B: `0.4327 [0.4144, 0.4502]`
   - bakeoff-Vanilla-3.5B: `0.4370 [0.4185, 0.4545]`

   - **iter-477 Vanilla-2B: `0.4792`** (point estimate, V4 CI pending re-emission for iter-477 entry)

It is also **above the bakeoff peak (bakeoff-Vanilla-3.5B at 0.4370)** by +4.22 pp, and **above bakeoff-Vanilla-5B (0.4305) at less than half the tokens**.
