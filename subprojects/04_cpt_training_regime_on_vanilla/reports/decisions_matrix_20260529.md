## Cross-check verdict

**Consistent.** No investigation directly contradicts any other. Three reinforcement points and two genuine gaps:

- **Reinforcement 1 — Matched-config eval is the only path to a defensible Vanilla-vs-Apertus-Base claim.** The V4 CI table cleanly shows Apertus-Base is +4.26 to +5.13 pp above every continued arm with CI excluding zero, but those CIs are computed against an Apertus-Base eval taken at the published 65536/RoPE-12M geometry, while the continued arms ran at 4096/RoPE-500K. The matched-config eval design (Option A symlink + config override) is precisely what closes that confound. The two investigations agree that the V4 CI artifact must be re-emitted after the matched-config Apertus-Base eval lands.
- **Reinforcement 2 — Iter 119 readout intentionally conservative.** V4 says iter-119 point estimate above all three bakeoff Vanilla checkpoints but CIs span zero; matched-config design notes iter 477 is "the first stable-LR snapshot" because warmup ends at iter 287; Vanilla-1B critique already flagged iter 238 (and by inheritance iter 119) as mid-warmup. All three agree: do **not** read regime-fixed from iter 119 alone.
- **Reinforcement 3 — Comma-truncation bug cleared, with leftovers.** Audit confirms `BENCHMARKS=` was the only top-level-comma `--export` value across the three audited directories, but flags C2/C3 unquoted-heredoc fragility in the same class (latent, currently safe). RUN_LOG Decision A documents the live fix with pre-/post-fix SHAs.
- **Gap 1 — V4 artifact will be incomplete until iter-238/477 resubmits land and matched-config Apertus-Base eval lands.** V4 file explicitly carries a `pending` block for iter-238 (job 2422769) and iter-477 (job 2422770); neither investigation says when V4 will be re-run. Implied but not captured as a decision.
- **Gap 2 — C1 xfer-bound watcher + checksum sidecar will queue indefinitely behind the 2026-06-11 reservation.** Audit calls this the only finding that delays iter 834 / iter 1192; matched-config eval design routes correctly to `normal` but the in-flight watcher (`2417454`) and the checksum sidecar inside `submit_checkpoint_sidecars.sh:259` are unfixed. RUN_LOG does not record any patch.
