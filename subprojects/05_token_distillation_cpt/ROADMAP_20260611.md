# Roadmap → the ~60 B-new-token production run (pilot complete)

Date: 2026-06-11. The 13.5 B two-arm run was a **pilot to design the real
~60 B-new-token CPT**, not a shippable model. This roadmap reads the pilot for
what it tells us about scaling. Companion: `reports/` (perf + MMLU + per-language),
`CXI_NOSPACE_DEEP_DIVE`, `STATUS_CONFORMANCE`.

## 1 · What the pilot proved (its job is done)

- **TD/Greek-tokenizer ≫ vanilla:** GreekMMLU base 48.8 → vanilla 55.6 → **TD 59.3**
  (decontaminated, clean). The 148,480 Greek tokenizer + Token Distillation is the
  production recipe; the vanilla control did its job and can be dropped at 60 B.
- **Infra solved + fast:** multi-node CXI works (`NCCL_NET_FORCE_FLUSH=0` fix),
  64×GH200 ≈ 96 % DP-efficient, ~8 h / 13.5 B, 0 NaN.
- **Retention is cheap and replay over-provisioned:** every replayed language held
  (worst −0.6 pp at 62–130 M tok/lang); only un-replayed distant languages fell.

## 2 · Corpus inventory — 60 B is data-feasible

Unique Greek in `selected_after_apertus_and_internal_dedup.parquet` (47.1 M docs):

| Source | Docs | ~Unique ext-tokens |
|---|---:|---:|
| HPLT ell_Grek clean60 | 46.5 M | **~41.4 B** |
| OpenArchives (academic) | 147 k | ~7.1 B |
| greek_phd (theses) | 35.8 k | ~5.0 B |
| Tail: eurlex + greek_legal_code, OpenSubtitles, parliament, Kallipos/Wikisource/openbook books, finewiki, Pergamos | ~0.5 M | ~1.5 B |
| **Total unique unseen Greek** | | **~55 B** |

**The pilot used only 10 B (~18 %).** So a ~60 B-new-token run ≈ **~1.1 epochs of
the full pool** — overwhelmingly fresh data. **The pilot's saturation was a budget
choice, not a data wall**; the "more epochs don't help" result does *not* cap 60 B.

## 3 · The saturation finding, reinterpreted

The pilot plateaued (GreekMMLU + held-out loss) by ~9–10 B = ~1 epoch of the *10 B
it was fed*. With ~55 B unique available, scaling to 60 B is mostly new tokens, so
we expect continued gains. **One thing to verify, not assume** (partial cooldown
confound): a mid-scale probe on the full corpus before the full spend (§6).

## 4 · The 60 B data plan (three decisions)

1. **HPLT dominates; OA is scarce.** HPLT = 41 B (75 % of unique); OpenArchives =
   7 B. The pilot's 70/30 can't hold from unique data. **Decision DA1:** let HPLT
   run ~75 % (1 epoch) and OA/phd take their natural ~13/9 %, **or** repeat OA ~2–3×
   to hold its share (OA drove the STEM/professional gains — quality vs uniqueness).
2. **Use the untapped tail.** eurlex + greek_legal_code → **Greek Law** (our weakest
   subject, +2.6); Kallipos/Wikisource/openbook → **literature**; OpenSubtitles →
   conversational; parliament → formal. ~1.5 B *unique* tokens that *also target the
   pilot's weak spots*. **Decision DA2:** include the tail (recommended) — re-clean +
   decontaminate it first.
3. **Cut replay.** Over-provisioned (§1). **Decision DA3 — SETTLED 2026-06-12 by the
   `curriculum_sweeps_v2` replay sweep → `PRODUCTION_MIX_DECISION_20260612.md`.** Result:
   **20 % foreign replay (R=0.25)**, NOT the 10–15 % guessed here — the sweep showed
   ~13 % (R=0.15) *hurts* adaptation while 0.25 ≈ 0.35 tie at the top; forgetting is flat
   across the range. Plus **old-Greek replay 5 %→1 %** (old Greek improves, never forgotten).
   Keep Balkan/major tiers + distribution-matched (StarCoderData, not CodeParrot).

## 5 · The 60 B recipe

- **Arm:** TD-148480 only (vanilla validated; optionally a tiny vanilla control at
  the probe scale, not at 60 B).
- **Schedule re-derived for the horizon** (~14,300 iters @ GBS 1024): warmup is a
  fixed iter-count → becomes a tiny fraction (fine); 20 % WSD cooldown of 60 B is
  large (~12 B) — confirm that's intended or shorten to 10–15 %.
- **Decontamination extended** to every reported benchmark (Global-MMLU, XNLI,
  XCOPA, English-MMLU, ARC, HellaSwag), not just GreekMMLU — screen *all* data incl.
  replay. Pilot gap; matters more at scale.
- **Drop the curriculum** (it never took effect — shuffle was on — and it's a
  confound; separability is covered by per-set evals).
- **HP** from the probe sweeps (§6).

## 6 · De-risk before the big spend — the probe (do this next)

One **~20–30 B run, TD arm, full corpus**, that does double duty:
- **Confirms scaling:** does GreekMMLU break past the pilot's ~59 % plateau with
  fresh data? (Expected yes.)
- **Carries the sweeps** (coordinate descent, ~6 B proxy legs, confirm at probe end):
  1. ~~Peak LR~~ — **DONE 2026-06-13** (`curriculum_sweeps_v2` sweep →
     `PRODUCTION_LR_DECISION_20260613.md`). Use **`5.5e-5`**. GreekMMLU alone
     preferred `1.1e-4`, but LM-loss adaptation/retention made `5.5e-5` the best
     overall loss-first choice.
  2. ~~Replay fraction~~ — **DONE 2026-06-12** (`curriculum_sweeps_v2` sweep → 20 % / R=0.25,
     old-Greek 1 %; `PRODUCTION_MIX_DECISION_20260612.md`). Drop from the probe grid.
  3. **AdEMAMix necessity** α {0 (=AdamW), 4, 8}.
- Cost ~16–24 h @ 64 GPU. Metric: decontaminated GreekMMLU + retention guardrail + BPB.

## 7 · Compute

60 B ≈ 6× the pilot → **~48 h/arm @ 64 GPU**, or **~24 h @ 128**. One arm (TD).
Plannable within a normal allocation; the FORCE_FLUSH-fixed CXI config is the
canonical launch recipe.

## 8 · Sequence + decisions

| Step | What | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Build the **full-corpus v2 dataset**: HPLT + OA + phd + **tail**, re-clean + **extended decontam**, replay per `PRODUCTION_MIX_DECISION_20260612.md` (79/20/1), curriculum TBD | DA1 (OA repeat?), DA2 (tail); ~~DA3~~ settled |
| 2 | **Probe (~20–30 B, TD)** = scaling check + α/cooldown sanity with settled replay + LR | AdEMAMix α grid; cooldown check |
| 3 | **Finalize 60 B recipe** from probe evidence; re-derive schedule | cooldown 20% vs shorter |
| 4 | **Run 60 B** (TD-148480), ~48 h @ 64 GPU | GPU count / allocation |

**Bottom line:** the pilot de-risked the recipe and the infra; the corpus inventory
clears the gating question (**~55 B unique Greek exists; the pilot used 18 %**). The
60 B run is a go on data grounds. Next concrete move is the **full-corpus build +
~20–30 B probe** that simultaneously proves the curve scales and tunes LR/replay —
then commit the 60 B run.
