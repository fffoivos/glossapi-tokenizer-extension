03 · Approximately 40B → endpoint

The scale proxy breaks exactly where checkpoint selection matters.

Only GPCR, Medical MCQA and OYXOY NLI share the same late direction. Mini continues to reduce GreekMMLU, ASEP and WSD choice NLL, while 8B reverses.

Medical MCQA replicates the peak

Both scales improve strongly before 40B and then worsen slightly. This is the cleanest evidence that Mini can sometimes predict checkpoint timing.

GPCR keeps learning

Both scales continue to improve after 40B, so a universal mid-run stop would sacrifice this capability.

GreekMMLU does not transfer

Mini reaches its best observed NLL at the endpoint, 1.2869. The 8B model peaks at 1.0740 near 40B and regresses to 1.1221.

DemosQA is nearly flat late

The exact signs differ, but the movements are only +0.00163 for Mini and -0.00009 for 8B. Without uncertainty estimates this is a numerical disagreement, not a strong scientific split.

08 · Interpretation

What Mini can tell us—and what it cannot.

Useful emergence screen

Six of nine continuous tasks move in the same direction by 40B. Mini can reject schedules that fail to produce broad early Greek learning.

Weak stopping-time proxy

Late direction agrees on only 3/9 tasks and best-checkpoint identity on 2/9. Mini should not decide when an 8B run stops or which checkpoint ships.

No macro winner

The tasks differ in size, class balance and semantics. Averaging them would let the 54k-item WiC panel dominate small but substantively different exams.

Not a causal scale study

Architecture, embedding tying, TD recipe, peak LR, RoPE scaling, sanitation, post-mask dedup and total token count all differ.

Preserve multiple checkpoints

The 8B ~40B model is strongest for GreekMMLU, Medical, NLI and WSD; the endpoint is strongest for GPCR and effectively tied on DemosQA.

Need denser native-Greek checkpoints

Three points establish that trajectories differ, but not the exact turn. The next study should score the existing intermediate checkpoints with paired uncertainty.

Decision rule: use Mini to screen whether a curriculum learns; use the target-scale validation panel and native-Greek suite to choose when to stop.
09 · Reproducibility

Every plotted number is receipt-bound.

The D0 matrix reused the canonical 8B frozen examples and scorer. The initial allocation preserved completed shards; the resume allocation computed only missing shards before fail-closed aggregation and strict rescoring.
