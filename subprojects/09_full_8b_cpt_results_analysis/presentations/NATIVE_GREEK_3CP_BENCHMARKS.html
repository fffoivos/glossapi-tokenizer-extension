01 · Experimental contract

One model trajectory, three frozen checkpoints, identical examples.

The screen asks whether the GreekMMLU peak near 40B is narrow or general. It does not compare data schedules or learning rates: model architecture, tokenizer, prompts, option order and evaluator arithmetic are held fixed.

Frozen examples

83,970 newly scored examples at every checkpoint, plus the existing 16,159-question clean GreekMMLU anchor. No checkpoint sees a different validation subset.

Base-model scoring

Zero-shot continuation likelihood with the validated FP32, candidate-batch-one evaluator. Accuracy is paired with choice NLL; OYXOY binary tasks also report balanced accuracy.

Two evidence views

Complete public splits remain visible. Strict-filtered scores remove only examples whose question/source surface and label-bearing second surface co-occur in a training document.

02 · Post-hoc contamination audit

Every exclusion points back to a dataset shard, row and line range.

The complete 51.84M-row published corpus was scanned. A question-only overlap is merely a candidate and is never automatically removed. A strict exclusion additionally requires the correct answer or paired source surface in the same document and proximity window.

The MCQ corrections are small

ASEP, DemosQA and Medical each lose at most 3.01% of examples. GPCR loses 6.73%. Their checkpoint ordering is stable after filtering.

Dictionary-derived OYXOY is different

32.27% of metaphor and 30.55% of WSD are excluded. The dominant source is the same Modern Greek dictionary from which these lexical judgments were constructed.

The filter is a reporting correction for an already-trained model. It does not pretend to undo training exposure, and it does not alter the published corpus.

04 · Class-balance check

Two striking raw accuracies collapse to chance after balancing.

Balanced accuracy gives equal weight to each class. It shows that the high initialization accuracy on OYXOY metaphor and WIC comes from predicting the majority class—not from superior linguistic judgment.

Metaphor

Raw initialization accuracy is 64.94%, but balanced accuracy is only 52.40%; the terminal model predicts one class exclusively and lands at exactly 50% balanced accuracy.

Words in context

Raw initialization accuracy is 74.37%, yet balanced accuracy is 49.86%. At 40B it reaches the best balanced value, 52.17% full and 52.44% filtered.

07 · Interpretation

What this screen changes—and what it does not.

Preserve the 40B checkpoint

It is the strongest single checkpoint across this screen: best on GreekMMLU, Medical MCQA, NLI and WSD, with the best choice NLL on ASEP as well.

The endpoint still has value

GPCR improves through the terminal checkpoint. ASEP and DemosQA are effectively flat-to-slightly-better at the endpoint, so late training is not uniformly destructive.

No macro “winner score”

These tasks differ in size, class balance, semantics and contamination. An unweighted mean would treat a 208-item commonsense test like a 58,831-item lexical test and hide the evidence.

The capability trade-off is real

Medical, NLI and WSD peak at 40B and then regress. This aligns with GreekMMLU’s post-peak decline even while source-conditioned language-model loss continued improving.

Filtering is not causal removal

The strict subset estimates performance away from identified source collisions. It cannot reconstruct the counterfactual model that would have been trained without those documents.

Protipa remains outside scope

Repository metadata is visible, but the authenticated Parquet download still returns HTTP 403 pending manual approval. No score or contamination claim is inferred without the rows.
