Five points expose local shape around the GreekMMLU maximum. They do not establish statistical significance, causality or a globally optimal stopping point.

02 · Why this window

GreekMMLU supplies the anchor—and peaks sharply at 40B.

The five new checkpoint positions were chosen around the best decontaminated GreekMMLU checkpoint. Accuracy and continuous choice loss agree on the location of that local optimum.

A broader evaluation should reproduce this 40B optimum if GreekMMLU is tracking a general capability peak. It does not: the surrounding tasks fan out in different directions.

05 · Class-balance diagnostics

WiC and NLI raw accuracy partly measure answer-label preference.

Balanced accuracy gives equal weight to both classes. GPCR shows a coherent learning trajectory; metaphor declines toward chance; NLI improves in balanced terms even while raw accuracy falls; WiC’s dramatic raw spike largely disappears after balancing.

GPCR: a credible late gain

Raw and balanced accuracy rise together from roughly 58% to 64%, peaking at 45B. Choice NLL continues improving at 50B, so the signal is not merely a threshold crossing.

NLI: capability and label bias move differently

Raw item accuracy falls from 63.4% to 59.9%, but balanced accuracy rises from 50.1% to 56.8% at 45B and exact-set accuracy rises from 2.2% to 26.5%.

WiC: the 45B spike is fragile

Raw accuracy jumps to 67.9% and then collapses to 33.8%. Balanced accuracy only moves between 50.1% and 55.0%, consistent with checkpoint-dependent answer-label preference.

Metaphor: a real regression

Balanced accuracy falls from 60.6% at 30B to chance at 50B, while choice NLL worsens from 0.719 to 1.355. Both discrete and continuous evidence agree.

07 · Scientific reading

The trajectory is multi-objective, not uniformly improving or forgetting.

Later training still teaches useful behavior

ASEP accuracy and NLL improve through 50B. GPCR accuracy peaks at 45B and NLL at 50B. Medical accuracy is noisy, but its NLL improves substantially by 50B.

Later training also damages specific behavior

Metaphor judgment declines across the entire window. GreekMMLU also moves away from its 40B optimum, so continued reduction of the language-model objective is not a guarantee of downstream improvement.

Raw binary accuracy can mislead

WiC and NLI change their answer-label preference across checkpoints. Balanced accuracy, macro F1 and NLL are required before interpreting a dramatic raw score as capability.

There is no defensible macro winner yet

Benchmark sizes range from 194 to 54,217 examples and measure different constructs. A pooled or unweighted average would create an arbitrary objective and hide the trade-offs.

Observed best is not statistical significance

These are paired deterministic evaluations of one training seed. Confidence intervals and additional seeds are needed before treating small differences—especially on GPCR, DemosQA or Medical—as stable.

Checkpoint selection should follow intended use

Preserve 30B for metaphor, 40B for GreekMMLU, 45B for GPCR/WiC diagnostics and 50B for ASEP/NLI exact-set. A single export discards evidence that is useful for later mixture and LR experiments.
