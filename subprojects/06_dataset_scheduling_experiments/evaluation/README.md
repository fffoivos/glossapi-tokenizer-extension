# 06 · evaluation — 13 panels, 415 GreekMMLU checkpoints, and the selection that stopped short of a winner

> **In one line:** the measurement half of the screen — thirteen source-conditioned heldouts, a natively authored GreekMMLU evaluator run at every checkpoint in every arm, a reconstructed retention suite, and the analysis that named D0 the observed leader while refusing to declare a statistical winner.
> **Period:** 2026-08-02 → 2026-08-05. **Status:** completed; 415/415 GreekMMLU and 5,395/5,395 validation bindings.
> **Came from / led to:** the panel design in [`../FACTORIAL_EXPERIMENT_DESIGN.md`](../FACTORIAL_EXPERIMENT_DESIGN.md) §§6–8 → this → [`../presentations/`](../presentations/) and the D0 decision recorded in [`../../07_full_8b_cpt/configs/owner_decisions_20260805.json`](../../07_full_8b_cpt/configs/owner_decisions_20260805.json).

## Why this existed

A schedule is not a winner because it ends with low loss on the pool it saw last. The design therefore fixed, before training, a set of source-conditioned panels (HPLT, aggregate GlossAPI/non-HPLT, per-family GlossAPI, neutral external Greek, per-language foreign replay, Old Greek, added-token-stratified), a continuous-metric benchmark policy, and a lexicographic selection rule — so that no post-hoc metric could be chosen to favour an arm.

## History

1. **Panels frozen (08-02).** [`build_validation_manifest.py`](build_validation_manifest.py) froze the 12 prepared heldouts plus one neutral external Greek panel — 13 in total, 2,097,152 loss-active tokens per fast panel, identical examples at every checkpoint, Goldfish masking off. The neutral panel itself was built by [`prepare_greek_parliament_neutral_source.py`](prepare_greek_parliament_neutral_source.py) → [`cross_deduplicate_neutral_external.py`](cross_deduplicate_neutral_external.py) → [`finalize_neutral_external_corpus.py`](finalize_neutral_external_corpus.py) → [`build_neutral_external_binary.py`](build_neutral_external_binary.py); the first cross-dedup pass mis-clustered 512 sittings into one component and was corrected before freezing 345 clusters / 15,057,527 tokens.
2. **GreekMMLU made trajectory-wide.** [`build_checkpoint_evaluation_plan.py`](build_checkpoint_evaluation_plan.py) produced the exact cadence — 83 checkpoints per arm, 415 evaluations — and [`build_greekmmlu_clean_subset.py`](build_greekmmlu_clean_subset.py) froze the decontaminated subset at 16,159 of 16,632 items. Each result is bound to an exact converted checkpoint by [`checkpoint_eval_export_receipt.py`](checkpoint_eval_export_receipt.py), [`verify_exact_checkpoint_weight_mapping.py`](verify_exact_checkpoint_weight_mapping.py) and [`finalize_checkpoint_greekmmlu_receipt.py`](finalize_checkpoint_greekmmlu_receipt.py) — the evaluator never treats a Megatron directory as an HF model.
3. **Retention suite rebuilt, not reused (08-03).** The prior lm-eval target directory had been stripped of its sources, so [`freeze_lm_eval_runtime.py`](freeze_lm_eval_runtime.py) and [`freeze_retention_dataset_cache.py`](freeze_retention_dataset_cache.py) reconstructed official `lm-eval==0.4.11` with a transparent `global_mmlu` alias over the 15 language-level groups ([`lm_eval_custom_tasks/`](lm_eval_custom_tasks/), [`lm_eval_runtime_requirements_0_4_11.txt`](lm_eval_runtime_requirements_0_4_11.txt)). Within-round comparisons are exact; comparisons to older reported values are labelled evaluator-reconstructed.
4. **A conversion-precision problem, and its fix.** The authoritative evaluation dtype was set to **float32** in namespace `fp32_v1`, and [`runtime_patches/`](runtime_patches/) holds the five diagnostic/parity patches used to establish that (bf16 semantic parity, fp32 semantic-parity identity, sub-argmax-threshold parity recording, top-logprob quantile diagnostic, and the `saver_swissai_hf` parity patch). The recovery finalizers live in [`../production/`](../production/).
5. **Endpoints (08-04/05).** [`build_endpoint_wave_manifest.py`](build_endpoint_wave_manifest.py) resolved the five terminal HF exports; [`finalize_full_endpoint_validation.py`](finalize_full_endpoint_validation.py) froze the 5-arm × 13-source panel (65 rows, 10,485,760 scheduled tokens/panel) at iteration 38,496; [`run_greek_endpoint_mcq_eval.py`](run_greek_endpoint_mcq_eval.py) and [`finalize_endpoint_wave.py`](finalize_endpoint_wave.py) added Greek Belebele and DemosQA; [`finalize_retention_endpoint_shard.py`](finalize_retention_endpoint_shard.py)/[`..._wave.py`](finalize_retention_endpoint_wave.py) produced the nine-task general retention endpoints.
6. **Selection (08-05).** [`analyze_dataset_order_selection.py`](analyze_dataset_order_selection.py) applied a 5% source-retention safety screen then the predeclared hierarchy (neutral-external Greek BPB → balanced HPLT/GlossAPI → GlossAPI macro), with 10,000-sample paired-question bootstraps and exact McNemar tests. [`summarize_core_campaign.py`](summarize_core_campaign.py) deliberately summarizes learning, forgetting and GreekMMLU endpoints *without* selecting a winner. [`collect_validation_trajectory.py`](collect_validation_trajectory.py) and [`collect_greekmmlu_trajectory.py`](collect_greekmmlu_trajectory.py) assembled the complete series.

## Outcome

- Complete evidence: **415/415** GreekMMLU checkpoint evaluations, **5,395/5,395** validation rows, 65 full-endpoint rows, five Greek-benchmark and five retention endpoint receipts.
- Screen result: D2 and D4 failed the 5% safety screen (historical/polytonic +13.06% and +7.17% vs D0); among passers the order was **D0 → D3 → D1**. D0 endpoint metrics: neutral-external BPB 0.43346, balanced 0.45631, GlossAPI macro 0.48758, clean GreekMMLU choice NLL 1.28691.
- `winner_selected: false`, for two stated reasons: the frozen receipts store aggregate panel losses but no document-cluster rows, and no numeric general-benchmark non-inferiority margin was frozen before endpoints were seen. The 5% margin that *was* used is explicitly labelled a reused safety sensitivity from the LR smoke, not a separately predeclared final-winner margin.
- Open: re-running endpoint validation with per-document-cluster numerators and UTF-8-byte denominators — planned and costed in [`../../07_full_8b_cpt/configs/per_document_validation_estimate.json`](../../07_full_8b_cpt/configs/per_document_validation_estimate.json), never executed for the 0.5B endpoints.
